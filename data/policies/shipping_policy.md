# Shipping Policy

## Delivery timelines
Metro cities: 2 to 4 business days. Other cities: 4 to 7 business days.

## Delayed shipments
If an order marked shipped has not been delivered within 7 days, the customer may raise a
ticket. Support should share the status and, if the delay exceeds 10 days, offer cancellation
of undelivered items or escalate to the logistics team.

## Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge while they are placed or packed.
Once an order is shipped or delivered it cannot be cancelled; the customer must use the
return process instead.
