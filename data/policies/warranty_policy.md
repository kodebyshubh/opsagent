# Warranty Policy

## Coverage
Products carry the manufacturer warranty listed in the product catalog (warranty_months).
Warranty covers manufacturing defects, not physical or liquid damage.

## Claims after the return window
After the return window closes, defective products under warranty are repaired or replaced
by the vendor. Support should create a support ticket for the warranty claim instead of
issuing a refund.

## Items without warranty
Items with 0 warranty months are covered only by the return policy.
