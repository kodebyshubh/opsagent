# Refund and Return Policy (v2.0)

## Return window
Customers may request a return or refund within **30 days of delivery** for eligible items.
This replaces the older 15-day window published in 2024.

## Eligible categories
Electronics, home, fashion, fitness and books are generally returnable within the window
if the item is defective, damaged in transit, or not as described.
Product-specific rules in the product catalog (for example hygiene or final-sale items)
apply in addition to this general policy.

## Non-returnable items
Opened beauty and personal care products are not returnable.
Items explicitly marked as final sale in the catalog are not returnable unless they are
defective on arrival, in which case a replacement or refund may be offered.

## Refund amount and approvals
Refunds are issued for the amount actually paid for the returned item.
A refund can never exceed the order total minus refunds already issued for that order.
Any single refund above Rs 5,000.00 must be approved by an operations manager before it is issued.

## Refund method and timeline
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
The customer must be informed by email when a refund is issued.

## Restocking
Returned items that are unopened or in resellable condition are restocked. Defective items
are sent back to the vendor and a replacement unit may be added to inventory.
