# Returns FAQ (legacy)

## How long do I have to return an item?
You can return most items within **15 days of delivery**.

## Are electronics returnable?
Yes. Electronics including earphones and earbuds can be returned within 15 days if unused.

## How are refunds processed?
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 10 business days.

## Do refunds need approval?
Refunds above Rs 10,000.00 require supervisor approval.
